Sega 32X and Genesis / Mega Drive core for Analogue Pocket (work in progress).

Put your 32X BIOS files in Assets/32x/common: 32X_G_BIOS.BIN, 32X_M_BIOS.BIN,
32X_S_BIOS.BIN. Cartridge saves (SRAM/EEPROM) are kept. Settings: region,
6-button pad, audio filter, FM chip, composite blend and more.

Based on S32X_MiSTer by srg320 and the MiSTer team, with fx68k (Jorge Cwik),
jt12/jt89 (Jose Tejada), T80 and agg23's openFPGA utilities.
Written with Claude Code; tested on real Pockets.
https://github.com/armyofgnomes/32x-core
